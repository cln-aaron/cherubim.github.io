Why proof matters

No proof, no finding.

The difference that matters is what happens after a candidate vulnerability is spotted. Cherubim passes every one to a verification step that reproduces the exploit in an isolated sandbox and captures the evidence. A finding only reaches you once it has been demonstrated, never because a model felt confident.

  • Working exploit attached to every finding, so triage debate disappears and fixes ship.
  • No regex false positives. Detection is proven by execution, not pattern matching.
  • CVSS and CWE mapping on each issue, with the OWASP category and remediation.
  • Non-destructive by construction, with rate-limit protection so production stays up.

Finding lifecycle

  1. DiscoverAgent maps the surface and spots a candidate issue
  2. ReasonPlans an exploit path toward real impact
  3. VerifySandbox reproduces the exploit and captures proof
  4. ReportEvidence, CVSS, and remediation attached, severity graded

If it can't be reproduced, it doesn't ship. That is the rule.

Data protection

Your findings stay yours.

Cherubim is built for teams that cannot hand their vulnerabilities to just anyone. Isolation, encryption, and control over your own data are defaults, not add-ons.

  • Row-level isolation so one tenant's data is never visible to another.
  • Encryption in transit with TLS across every request and response.
  • No training on your data. Your engagements are never used to train models.
  • Export and delete on your terms, with full control over retention.

The kindest thing a guardian can do is show you the exploit before an attacker does, and prove it, so you fix the right thing.

Questions

Straight answers

How is this different from a scanner with an AI label?

A scanner reports possibilities. Cherubim reasons, exploits, and then proves the finding with a working exploit. If it cannot reproduce the issue, it does not become a finding, so you get evidence instead of a backlog of maybes.

Is it safe to run against production?

Yes. Payloads are non-destructive and rate-limit protection is built in, so Cherubim is designed to run against live applications without taking them down.

How long does a scan take?

A single target typically completes in 10 to 30 minutes across all 22 phases. Wildcard mode discovers and scans subdomains, so it takes longer depending on how much it finds.

Can I automate it?

Yes. A REST API, a GitHub app, CI/CD gating, and scheduled scans let you run Cherubim on every release and break the build only on proven findings above your chosen severity.
